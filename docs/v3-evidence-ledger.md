# V3 · Evidence-led portfolio release ledger
As of 21 October 2026. **Public-facing audit of what can actually be defended.**

## Executive thesis
I lead the engineering of reviewable AI systems, from legacy-source modernization to agent-assisted software delivery. The buyer must be able to inspect **responsibility → decision → test → result → boundary**, in that order. No metric is a salary predictor.

## Four proof tiers
| Tier | What belongs here | Allowed claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 · Public executable | Repo source, fixture-backed automated CI on same SHA, diagrams with tests | Implemented and locally exercised under disclosed assumptions |
| E2 · Approved client evidence | Written customer/manager signoff, anonymized deployment logs, metrics with denominators | Bounded reported delivery; independently verified only with authorization |
| E3 · Team account | Participant recollections, partial artifact archive, unreleased internal test records | Team-reported engineering account, not third-party audited impact |
| E4 · Concept or mock | Browser concept, illustrative diagram, synthetic data | Concept or historical prototype only |

## Metrics editorial policy
- **No 89.9% on the home or Work headline** without a published measurement denominator, unit, query, corpus date, formula and reproducible baseline.
- Four-week PoC is a duration of a bounded test, never a complete deployment.
- `13 gates` is a reported technical-check total; publish list, scope and run output before using it as an independent certification.
- `250+` at Ayesa describes **organisational scale**, not 250 measured adopters.
- `201` co-evolution *records* are not 201 shipped products; 140 Confirmed, 58 Strong recall, 3 Partial reconstruction refer to internal provenance, not independent audit.
- CI green only certifies its exact SHA and supported environments; never infer real-world uptime.
- No direct customer branding or private engineering source unless employer and client approve publication.

## Promotion gates
**P0:** Homepage references no unverified instrumentor as primary evidence; engineering proof pages link to real repos, current CI and measured boundaries; source/build/browser checks pass.
**P1:** At least two signed-off case evidence cards contain personal ownership, intervention date, sample/baseline, output, errors and business response. Anonymous approved reference quote if possible.
**P2:** Five real external readers complete 5/30-second test; core web vitals from representative production traffic; administrative domain ownership reconciled with rollback.

## Status language
- **Built / implemented**: public repository with source.
- **Tested locally**: fixture-backed scenario plus SHA-specific test.
- **Team reported**: account from participants, not independently audited.
- **Approved client evidence**: explicit permission and source with date and denominator.
- **Production**: real deployment, production IAM, operational logs and owner acknowledgement. `Vercel READY` only proves the web artifact was deployed.

## Nonpublic capture protocol
Use `docs/v3-evidence-request-kit.md` to solicit safe aggregates and authorization. Do not copy client code, tickets, employee data or source documents into the public repository. All future figures in case pages must point to a reviewed source record.

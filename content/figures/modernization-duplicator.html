Reproducible copies are not a migration

In a banking PoC I designed a factory that reads legacy code with source lineage and generates artifacts from one model. This duplicator shows what was demonstrated and what remains unproven.

Speed

    What it shows

    • 22,995 records linked to file, line and SHA-256, out of 23,811 registry entries.
    • Two runs compared across 4,338 files with no defects under the defined reproducibility check.
    • A corpus-first approach: understand, normalize and trace before generation.

    What it does not claim

    • No demonstrated functional parity with the legacy system.
    • No complete migration or production rollout.
    • No verified savings or bank-wide applicability.
